Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ last_updated }}

This Privacy Policy explains what information GoonBot collects, how it is used, and the rights you have over it.

1. Data Controller

GoonBot is operated by its developer ("we", "us"). For any privacy questions, use the contact details at the bottom of this page.

2. Information We Collect

GoonBot collects and stores the following data:

  • Discord identity — your user ID, username, and avatar, obtained when you use the Bot or sign into the Dashboard via Discord OAuth.
  • Economy data — your virtual coin balance, daily reward claims and streaks, gambling warnings, and any temporary gambling lockout.
  • Music data — songs you add (title, artist, and link), your music ELO score, battle and vote history, and Song of the Day results.
  • Saved messages — messages you save with /message_add.
  • Dashboard messages — messages you send through the Dashboard, stored in a log visible to the server administrator.
  • Suggestions — suggestions you submit with /suggest, posted to the configured suggestions channel.
  • Message content in the music channel — GoonBot reads messages posted in the configured music channel solely to detect music links and add them to the sender's collection. It does not store the message itself, only the resulting song metadata.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide the Bot's features (games, economy, music, and the Dashboard);
  • Enforce moderation (gambling warnings and lockouts);
  • Verify that Dashboard users are members of the server;
  • Operate and improve the Bot.

4. Legal Basis

We process this data on the basis of our legitimate interest in operating the Bot and providing its features and, where required by law, your consent.

5. Storage & Retention

Data is stored in a database on the server that hosts GoonBot. It is retained for as long as you use the Bot and may be kept for moderation or operational purposes. If you leave the server, your Dashboard access is revoked automatically, though some historical records (such as message logs or music battle history) may be retained.

6. Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information. Your data is shared only:

  • With Discord, as necessary for the Bot and Dashboard login to function;
  • With music platforms (for example YouTube or Spotify) when the Bot retrieves song metadata for a link you provide.

We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers.

7. Message Content Intent

GoonBot enables Discord's "Message Content" privileged intent, which allows it to read the text of messages. This is used only in the channel configured as the music channel, to detect shared music links. It does not mean we read or log your conversations in other channels.

8. Cookies

The Dashboard uses a session cookie to keep you signed in. It is removed when you log out or close your browser.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. You can also revoke the Bot's access or remove it from your Discord account at any time.

10. Children's Privacy

GoonBot is not directed at children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the Bot, contact us and we will delete it.
